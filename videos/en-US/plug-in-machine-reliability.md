# Video Overview

A successful automatic insertion demonstration proves that the machine completed the current action, but it does not prove long-term reliability. Reliable equipment must maintain insertion position, force and motion consistency across batches, long runs and real production cycle times.

## What the Video Shows

The machine inserts component leads into PCB holes. The key observations are insertion-head positioning, component orientation and the final insertion result.

## How Reliability Should Be Verified

1. Does the insertion position remain consistent across repeated cycles?
2. Do accuracy and cycle time change after continuous operation?
3. Can the machine remain stable after component or batch changes?
4. Can it detect and recover from jams or positional errors?
5. Are wear and maintenance frequency controllable over long-term use?

## Practical View

Good automation should not be judged by one impressive demonstration. Its value should be proven through stable, repeatable production results, with accuracy, reliability and maintainability evaluated together.
