# Video Overview

When line output is insufficient, the slowest individual machine is not always the cause. Loading, transfer, buffering and process connections can also create waiting time and prevent high-performance equipment from reaching practical capacity.

## What the Video Shows

The video presents a dual-lane conveyor in an SMT line. Two transfer channels coordinate PCB flow and provide a flexible connection for parallel production, processes with different cycle times and temporary buffering.

## Why One Machine Is Not the Only Bottleneck

1. Upstream and downstream equipment operate at different cycle times.
2. A single transfer channel limits parallel process capacity.
3. Without buffering, a short stop can interrupt the entire line.
4. Frequent manual intervention breaks continuous production flow.

## Role of a Dual-Lane Conveyor

- Supports synchronized or independent transfer on two channels.
- Balances cycle times between upstream and downstream equipment.
- Reduces waiting and unnecessary line stops.
- Creates routing flexibility for parallel lines, rework and product changes.

## Practical View

Increasing output does not always require replacing the main machine. Identifying the real line constraint and improving process connections or material flow can often deliver a better result with a smaller change.
