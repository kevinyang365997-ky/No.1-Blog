# Video Overview

AOI and visual inspection find many surface defects, but solder joints beneath BGA and QFN packages cannot be seen directly. X-Ray inspection provides an internal view and stronger evidence for evaluating hidden solder connections.

## What X-Ray Can Inspect

- Hidden joints under BGA, CSP and QFN packages
- Voids, insufficient solder, excess solder, bridges and open-joint risks
- Internal PCB structures, through-hole fill and some assembly defects
- Products requiring failure analysis, first-article approval or process validation

## When the Investment Is More Valuable

1. Products use many bottom-terminated packages that conventional inspection cannot cover.
2. Automotive, medical or aerospace products require higher reliability.
3. The cost of escaped defects and field repairs is greater than the inspection cost.
4. The factory needs process optimization, sampling or failure analysis.
5. Customers or quality systems require X-Ray inspection records.

## Not Every Line Needs to Buy One

For simple products with few hidden joints and low inspection frequency, outsourced inspection, shared equipment or offline sampling may be sufficient. The decision should consider risk, volume, inspection takt time and return on investment.

## Practical View

The value of X-Ray is not simply making inspection look more advanced. It is the ability to see defects that would otherwise remain hidden when those risks can affect quality, delivery or customer confidence.
