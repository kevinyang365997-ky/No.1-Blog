# Video Overview

A component that does not fit a standard feeder is not necessarily unsuitable for automation. Components with unusual shapes, strict orientation requirements or non-standard packaging can be handled with a dedicated feeding mechanism designed around the real process.

## Common Challenges

1. The component size or geometry falls outside the range of a standard feeder.
2. Irregular incoming packaging makes orientation difficult to control.
3. Manual placement creates unstable cycle times and positioning variation.
4. Standard equipment cannot connect directly with the existing assembly process.

## Value of a Custom Solution

- Locating, separating and conveying mechanisms are designed for the component.
- Orientation and pickup position remain consistent.
- Manual handling and repeated adjustments are reduced.
- Feeding reliability and overall production rhythm can improve.
