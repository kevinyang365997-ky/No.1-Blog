# Video Overview

Standard feeding equipment does not suit every component. For odd-form parts, special packaging and application-specific cycle times, a custom SMT feeder can create a more stable and accurate supply process that integrates with the existing automation line.

## Main Features

1. Designed around component geometry, packaging and feeding direction.
2. Improves repeatable feeding accuracy and reduces jams or position errors.
3. Supports stable operation with less frequent manual intervention.
4. Connects flexibly with assembly, inspection and other automation processes.

## Typical Applications

- Automatic feeding of odd-form electronic components
- Separation and transfer of specially packaged materials
- Feeding for custom assembly equipment
- Processes that require better cycle time and consistency
